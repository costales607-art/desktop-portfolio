What do you actually build?

CRM setup, A2P registration, multi-step workflow automation, GoHighLevel to ClickUp request and billing systems, webhook routing, Google Apps Script tools, data cleanup and migration, and spreadsheet reporting.

Can you work inside an existing GoHighLevel account?

Yes. I configure, troubleshoot and improve live accounts: sub-account setup, smart lists, pipeline logic, integrations, and SMS deliverability problems such as opt-out and error-limit pauses.

Do you handle data migration and cleanup?

Yes. I build cleaning and mapping routines for messy CSV exports so contacts import into the right CRM fields without manual stripping.

How quickly do you learn a new platform?

Fast, and I document what I build. In a few months I became independent on GoHighLevel, including A2P registration and a ClickUp-connected billing system.
